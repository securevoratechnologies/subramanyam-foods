Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour & Hospitality

At Subramanyam Foods, we believe that great food begins with respect — for tradition, for ingredients, and for the people we serve. Every dish is prepared fresh, using recipes passed down through generations and spices sourced with care.

From our kitchen in Hebbal to your table, we invite you to experience the warmth of Indian hospitality in every bite.
